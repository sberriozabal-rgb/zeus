El cambio de orden que importa

El comprador de una skill es alguien que ya usa Claude Code. Un DJ que además usa Claude es una intersección pequeña; un restaurante que use Claude, más pequeña aún. Por eso el orden de empuje es el contrario al del catálogo:

La skill gratuita es el anzuelo en todos los canales: dos líneas para instalarla y ver cómo trabaja una pieza de la serie.

Tres reglas que no se tocan

  1. Decir que es tuyo. Todas las comunidades aceptan «I built…» y castigan el anuncio disfrazado. Un post, una vez, y se contesta a quien comente.
  2. Ninguna promesa ni cifra sin fuente. Las de la doctrina. Y no decir «probadas».
  3. Un enlace por post: el repositorio público. Los precios van en un comentario propio o en la ficha, no en el título.

Treinta segundos antes de publicar nada

  • Topics del repositorio (Settings del repo → ⚙ junto a About → Topics): claude-code claude-skills agent-skills claude dj rekordbox restaurant accounts-receivable plugin-marketplace spanish. Es lo que hace que aparezca en las búsquedas de GitHub. La fábrica no puede ponerlos (403 del proxy).
  • Gumroad → Settings → Discover: apagado en los 15 productos. Si una venta entra por el buscador de Gumroad, la comisión es el 30 %.

1 · r/ClaudeAI y r/ClaudeCode (inglés) · el primero que sale

Title: I turned my consulting work into Claude Code skills. One is free (restaurant shift checklists), the rest are paid. Here's what they do and don't do.

Body:

I'm a consultant (restaurants, DJ booths, B2B collections). Over the summer I rewrote the procedures I actually use as Claude Code skills, following the open Agent Skills spec. Each one reads your own data (a rekordbox XML, an invoice list, a POS export), runs a procedure with declared thresholds, and says what it does NOT do.

Free, installable now:

/plugin marketplace add sberriozabal-rgb/octava-skills
/plugin install octava-abiertas@octava

That's apertura-cierre-turno: opening / handover / closing checklists for a restaurant, with an owner per task, exact time and a "done" criterion, plus the incident report. Spanish-language output (that's my market), but the structure is the point.

Paid (17 in total, fully documented in the repo):

  • Context pack for anyone with months of work inside a Claude project: one skill exports it to an encrypted bundle with a rebuild script, the other turns it into an ordered library with "the list of what you haven't decided yet". It does not migrate your account; the platform doesn't allow it.
  • Overdue receivables plan: priority by age × cause, the messages already written, escalation dates, DSO dashboard. B2B only, not legal advice.
  • Six DJ-booth skills: library audit by booth risk, gig post-mortem from the exported history, set-for-a-slot, request lists, quote + contract clauses, demo submission timing. They read exports; they never write to rekordbox or Serato.

Every skill ships with four test cases and a "what it doesn't do" section, because I'd rather lose a sale than get a refund. Repo with every sheet and price: https://github.com/sberriozabal-rgb/octava-skills

Happy to answer anything about how they're structured (the frontmatter, the procedure template, the validator) — that part is reusable for anyone writing their own.

Comentario propio, nada más publicar: Prices, since people ask: singles 49 €, DJ bundle of six 249 €, receivables 79 €, context pack 89 €. Gumroad, one-time, no subscription.

Title: I built a tool that reads your rekordbox XML and tells you which tracks will fail at your next gig, in what order to fix them

Body:

Not a cleaner. Lexicon cleans; this doesn't touch your library. It reads the exported collection.xml and ranks what will actually hurt you in the booth: broken paths and missing beatgrids first, missing ratings last, and a Top 3 for the gig date you give it.

It runs inside Claude Code as a "skill" (a folder with a procedure the model follows). If you don't use Claude Code, this isn't for you yet, and I'd rather say so.

Offer, no strings: send me your XML and I'll send back the three most urgent things, free. If it's useful, there's a paid version with five more booth skills (gig post-mortem from your history export, set for a given slot, request lists, quotes + contract clauses, demo timing). Sheets and prices: https://github.com/sberriozabal-rgb/octava-skills

5 · LinkedIn (español) · cobro de cartera vencida

¿Tienes facturas vencidas y no sabes por cuál empezar?

Convertí el protocolo de cobro que uso en consultoría en una skill para Claude. Le pasas el listado y te devuelve: a quién reclamar primero y por qué causa —no es lo mismo el que discute la factura que el que no tiene la orden de compra—, el mensaje redactado para cada uno, y la fecha exacta en la que subes el tono si no paga.

Lo que más recupera no es reclamar antes: es dejar de reclamar sobre facturas mal emitidas. Mientras el papel está mal, el reloj no corre a tu favor.

No es asesoría jurídica y no sirve para cobrar a particulares. 79 €, pago único. Ficha completa en el primer comentario.

Primer comentario: https://github.com/sberriozabal-rgb/octava-skills/blob/main/catalogo/cobro-cartera-vencida.md
